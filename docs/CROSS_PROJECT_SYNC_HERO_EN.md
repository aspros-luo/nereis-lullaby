# Cross-Project Sync Note: Nereis-Hero Maps and Shared Worldbuilding

> Date: 2026-10-12  
> Status: Synchronization memo; no new canon established  
> Project: Nereis Lullaby / 蜜忒之歌

## 1. Synchronization rules

- Nereis-Hero and Nereis Lullaby are independent games with different gameplay and viewpoints, sharing only established world facts.
- Maps, combat systems, provisional items, and debug scenes in the Hero repository are implementation details. They do not automatically become Lullaby canon.
- Only facts explicitly approved by the user may move from “Proposal / Open” to “Confirmed.”
- Maintain Chinese and English design documents together. Do not trigger GitHub Actions.

## 2. Shared confirmed direction

- Mite's innocence and emotions are genuine; she does not consciously understand her relationship to the Outer God in ordinary human terms.
- Church research, ancient-god residue, the Kingdom's faith system, and Outer-God influence are related but distinct layers of power.
- Villagers have individual lives, relationships, and motives; participating in an anomaly does not mean everyone understands the ritual.
- The games may differ in viewpoint and information. What the player knows does not automatically become knowledge held by Mite or Hero.
- Shared worldbuilding does not require both games to repeat the same scenes, but character knowledge, timeline, and causality must remain consistent.

## 3. Current Hero map implementation: development facts only

The Hero development branch is reviewing the interconnected box-world structure of A0–A12 and the eight canonical hidden regions H01–H08. Current focus includes:

- A2–A4: forest high routes, abandoned city, sewer / reservoir, and loops;
- A5–A8: battlefield, monastery, prison, and royal-tomb spatial layers;
- A9–A12: royal-city outer ring, cathedral, deep research area, and inner city;
- Resonance requirements, hidden entrance discovery, return routes, and persistent state.

These are level-layout and implementation facts. They do not mean Lullaby must feature these locations or events. Environmental clues become shared narrative facts only after their story meaning is explicitly approved.

## 4. Keep these topics as proposals / unresolved

- Whether the ritual center is inside the village, deep in the forest, or distributed across both.
- Whether villagers collectively become cultists to protect Mite; if so, when, why, and which ending it belongs to.
- Hero's and Mite's eventual fates and whether the games have an explicit causal bridge.
- Whether the restoration army becomes corrupt, and the story significance of provisional H09 and its relic.
- Final conditions and cross-game causal chains for 凡人之墓, 永恒乐园, and 烈火天罚.

The Hero repository labels H09's candidate cave and relic as provisional map content and decouples them from ending eligibility. Lullaby must not treat them as established world facts.

## 5. Future sync workflow

Whenever either project adds a major plot event, item, character-knowledge fact, or timeline detail:

1. Classify it as Confirmed, Proposal, or Open.
2. Check whether it creates a character-knowledge, timeline, or causality conflict in the other project.
3. Synchronize only the necessary shared facts; do not impose one game's gameplay rules on the other.
4. Update Chinese and English documents together.
5. Keep unapproved content labeled as a proposal rather than presenting it as final canon.

## 6. Scope

This file is a cross-project synchronization memo. It does not approve new ending details, ritual mechanics, or character fates, and does not replace either game's design contracts.
