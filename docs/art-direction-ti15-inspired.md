# Nereis Lullaby — Art Direction

## Reference

The character presentation direction takes inspiration from the polished fantasy portrait language associated with Dota 2 The International 2025/2026 promotional presentation: strong character silhouettes, dramatic rim lighting, restrained dark backgrounds, readable faces, and premium portrait-card composition. The International's official 2025 media archive contains extensive event photography and visual material, while the tournament campaign used digitally painted backgrounds, custom iconography, typography, and illustration as a coordinated visual system.

Reference sources:
- https://www.flickr.com/photos/dota2ti/albums/72177720328958190/

## Nereis Lullaby adaptation

This is a stylistic reference, not a reproduction of Dota 2 assets or characters.

### Character portraits

- Three-quarter or bust portrait rather than flat RPG sprites.
- Strong silhouette and immediately readable facial expression.
- Directional key light plus subtle rim light.
- Dark, low-detail environment behind the character.
- Warm skin/material highlights against cool or desaturated surroundings.
- Character-specific accent color should be restrained and used for story readability.
- Portrait framing should leave negative space for dialogue UI.

### Current NPC identities

- Hunter: weathered, practical, restrained expression; muted forest green/brown accents.
- Merchant: confident, layered clothing, warm leather/gold accents; more open expression.
- Hero: pale, uncertain, slightly uncanny; cooler desaturated palette and subtle unnatural highlights.

### UI

- Deep charcoal / burgundy surfaces.
- Aged parchment text.
- Warm muted gold for important interactive elements.
- No bright fantasy-game neon UI.
- Hover feedback should be small scale/position changes rather than large animations.
- Story choices should feel like deliberate decisions, not ordinary menu buttons.

### Narrative presentation

The art direction should become progressively less comfortable as the hidden story state advances. Early scenes remain warm and grounded; later scenes introduce colder lighting, stronger contrast, irregular shadows, and increasingly unnatural portrait treatment.

## Asset policy

Use original/generated assets for Nereis Lullaby. Do not copy Dota 2 hero portraits, Valve artwork, logos, or tournament assets into the game.
