# Village Faith and the “Blazing Divine Retribution” Story Addendum

> Status: core direction confirmed; character identities and exact presentation remain open.

## The village's peaceful surface
The village is broadly peaceful and harmonious. Local villagers live alongside outsiders displaced by war. The village must not be portrayed as a place where everyone is pretending to be ordinary cultists; everyday kindness, mutual aid, and genuine feelings can all exist.

## The few who know
A very small number of villagers know that Mite is important, but they do not know why and do not understand her true nature. They may be close to Mite and sincerely care for, look after, and protect her. Their hidden identities might include cult priests, bishops, or other figures, but their exact identities, number, offices, and relationships are not finalized.

Distinguish three levels of knowledge: knowing Mite is important, knowing the ritual's ultimate goal, and knowing that Mite is an agent of the Outer God. These are not interchangeable. Cult members do not all need to understand what their participation means.

## The ritual's goal
Confirmed: the cult ritual ultimately aims to bring the Outer God into the world. Its mechanics, stages, location, participant roles, and each person's awareness of the goal remain open.

## “Blazing Divine Retribution” ending
Confirmed story direction: after villagers learn that Mite is an agent of the Outer God and Mite breaks with the protagonist, they will resolutely protect what they believe in. The protected object may involve their faith itself, the ritual, or the Outer God's arrival; exact presentation remains open.

This must not turn villagers into personality-free enemies. They may understand the village, Mite, faith, and the protagonist differently; their affection for Mite may still be genuine. The conflict comes from a rupture in belief and choice, not a simplistic switch between good and evil factions.

## Writing constraints
- Do not portray every villager as a cultist.
- Do not portray every outsider as suspicious.
- Do not equate being close to Mite with knowing the truth or manipulating her.
- Do not finalize specific priest or bishop identities without later approval.
- Do not automatically extend this ending's villager allegiance to other endings.
